01 — Philosophy

The company for humans.

The goal is simple to say and hard to keep: build software for the person holding it, then build a company that can keep doing that as it grows.

  1. Monetization must never be invasive, annoying, or predatory. If a feature requires no real engineering to provide, it stays free.

  2. The books are open. Every source of income, every expense, every milestone — published where anyone can verify them.

  3. Every suggestion gets an answer. Built and shipped, or declined with a reason. Nothing disappears into a void.

These are not aspirations. They are operating constraints, written down — and you can hold Celtaury to them.

02 — Products

Cura

A phone cleaner that can’t phone home.

Cura finds what your phone accumulates — old screenshots, WhatsApp leftovers, oversized files — and deletes only what you approve. It has no INTERNET permission, which means it is not merely unwilling to send your data anywhere. It is unable to.

  • Quick Clean — one pass over the obvious junk

03 — Money & support

Honest money.

Celtaury is self-funded and built alongside university studies. It plans to grow on a declared ladder rather than on your attention — the same ladder, published, for every product.

  1. Donations from users who want to help.

  2. Memberships — early builds, credits, a closer look behind the scenes.

  3. Paid Pro unlocks — optional. The free tier always stays complete.

  4. Affiliate links — on this website and in video descriptions only, always disclosed. Never inside an app.

  5. Honest prices for future products that earn them.
