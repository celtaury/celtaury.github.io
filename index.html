01 — Philosophy

The company for humans.

The plan is simple: build software for the person holding it, then build a company that can keep doing that as it grows.

  1. Free stays free. If a feature doesn’t need real work to run, we don’t charge for it.

  2. Open books. Every rupiah in and out, published where anyone can check.

  3. You talk, we answer. Every suggestion gets built — or gets a reason.

Simple rules, written down — so you can hold us to them.

Fluid enough to move fast. Structured enough to keep promises.
Ambitious enough to grow. Grounded enough to say no.

03 — Money & support

Honest money.

Self-funded, built between classes. Here’s the whole plan — published, for every product.

  1. Donations — if you want to help.

  2. Memberships — early builds, your name in the credits.

  3. Pro unlocks — optional extras. Free stays complete.

  4. Affiliate links — this site and video descriptions only. Never inside an app.

  5. Real prices — for future products that earn them.
