04 — Rules

How to read this page.

  1. Updated by hand, in public. Every change is a commit anyone can diff. No silent edits — corrections stay in the history.

  2. Every row has a source. A receipt, a statement, or a dashboard. If a number can’t be sourced, it doesn’t go in.

  3. Estimates wear an asterisk. The * means an assumed rate, stated right under the table. Estimates get replaced — never quietly kept.

  4. IDR first, USD second. Money is recorded in the currency it actually moved in. Totals lead in rupiah, because this is an Indonesian company.

  5. The meter doesn’t count promises. Money in the account, or the needle doesn’t move. That’s the whole trick.
